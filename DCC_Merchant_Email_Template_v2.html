What is Dynamic Currency Conversion?

When an international cardholder pays at your terminal, DCC gives them the option to pay in their home currency instead of local currency. When they choose it, you earn a little extra on top of that payment — automatically, with no extra effort beyond a single question at the point of sale. It's one of the simplest ways to increase your margins.
